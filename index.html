Everything you need to stay on routine

Built for real life — for medicines, creams, meals, walks, workouts and everything in between.

Alarm-style reminders

Reminders ring over whatever you are doing — even on the lock screen — sharp at the set time. Choose notification-only, or ring + notification, with preset alarm tones or your own custom audio.

Every kind of routine

Medicines (11 types), creams and gels, meals, water, walks, cycling, gym, rest, sleep and to-dos. Group routines under one shared reminder time — each member still tracked on its own.

Cute capsule buddies

Every routine gets its own capsule illustration, guessed automatically from its name and time — or pick your favourite when you create it. Routines feel less like chores and more like company.

Today, at a glance

Big, easy-to-tap blocks with the name, time and state of each dose. Mark taken, snooze or skip in one tap — and opening a card never logs a dose by accident.

History you can trust

Every record is timestamped, with Undo for any entry. Archived routines stay in your history forever, and you can export everything as a CSV file — all of it, or a date range.

Private by design

No account, no sign-up, no internet needed. Your routines and records live only on your phone — never uploaded, never shared. And there are no ads, ever.

Good to know

Straight answers, no fine print.

Is Remibuds really free?

Yes — free to download, free to use, no ads, no subscriptions. Donations are optional and only keep the app running.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline, on your phone. Your data never leaves your device.

Is my information private?

Yes. There is no account and nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your routines and history stay on your phone, and uninstalling the app erases them — so export a backup first if you want to keep them.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet — Remibuds is Android-only for now.

Will it ring if my phone is switched off?

No — no alarm can ring a phone that is fully powered off. It rings reliably whenever the phone is on, even locked or in another app.
